From your surname to your family

Questions

Do I need to read Chinese?

No. Everything we find is explained and translated into English.

Can you always find my family's clan book?

Not always. Many books were lost, and some families never had one. That's why the first check is free: we tell you honestly what exists before you pay for anything.

My family spelled the name differently — is it the same surname?

Often, yes. Chan, Tan, Chin and Trần can all be 陈; Lee, Lí and Yi can be 李; Wong and Ng can be 黄 or 吴 depending on dialect. We work it out from the Chinese character and where the family came from.

Are the people in the films real portraits?

Historical figures are shown with public-domain paintings from museum collections. Where we use an AI-generated likeness, it is labelled on screen.
